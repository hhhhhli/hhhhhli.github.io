Li Huang (黄利)

Postdoctoral Researcher, Tsinghua University

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Xiang Li's IRM Lab at Tsinghua University. I received my Ph.D. degree from Zhejiang University under the supervision of Prof. Yaowu Chen and Prof. Xiang Tian. Before that, I received my B.Eng. degree from Zhejiang University under the supervision of Prof. Yu Zhang and Prof. Rong Xiong.

My research focuses on dual-arm dexterous manipulation, and deformable objects. I am open to collaborations, please feel free to contact me at huang_li@mail.tsinghua.edu.cn.

Research

My research focuses on enabling robots to reliably perceive and manipulate deformable objects in the real world, spanning perception, and dual-arm dexterous manipulation.

Research Keywords: Deformable Object Perception & Dual-arm Dexterous Manipulation, Robot Perception.
